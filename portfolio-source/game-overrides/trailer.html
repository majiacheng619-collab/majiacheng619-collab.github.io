四个世界Four worlds

从清晨到夜战，20 关逐步解锁。Sunlight, speed, sunset and fog pose four different questions on the same lawn. Start in the morning and discover the garden at your own pace.

01 · 清晨01 · Morning

从第一束阳光开始。Start with the first ray of sun.

更多天降阳光，留出认识植物与成长节奏的空间。Extra sky sun gives you room to learn your plants and their growth.

02 · 正午02 · Noon

把握每一次生长。Make every moment count.

来客脚步更快，火力与前排保护需要更早到位。Faster visitors demand earlier damage and frontline protection.

03 · 黄昏03 · Dusk

余晖渐暗，选择更重。Less light. Bigger decisions.

天降阳光减半，击退者让前排布局面临新的考验。Sky sun is halved, and pushers challenge your front line.

04 · 夜战04 · Night

先照亮，再守护。Light the way. Hold the line.

没有天降阳光，带上产出与光源，照亮迷雾中的目标。No sky sun. Bring income and light to reveal targets in the fog.
