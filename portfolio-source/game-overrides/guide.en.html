SPROUT PATROL · HOW TO PLAY

先种下。
再一起守住。Plant a little.
Protect a lot.

不用记住一堆规则。收集阳光，种下植物，挡住从右边走来的僵尸。第一关会一步步带你练习。No rulebook to memorise. Collect sun, plant your defenders, and stop the zombies approaching from the right. The first garden guides you through it.

这一局，要做什么？What am I trying to do?

守住左边的家。击败本关所有波次的僵尸即可过关；如果关卡显示额外目标，也要一起完成。僵尸突破最后一道防线，这一局就会结束。Protect the house on the left. Defeat every wave to finish the garden, and complete any extra objective shown for that level. A zombie getting past your last defence ends the attempt.

第一次玩，照着这四步。Your first game in four steps.

01

点一下阳光，攒下种植费用。Tap the sun. Save up for a plant.

草坪上亮起的金色阳光是你的种植费用。点一下就能收集，也可以点“收集阳光”一次收走全部。先收阳光，再买植物。The glowing golden suns pay for your plants. Tap one to collect it, or use Collect sun to gather them all. Collect first, then plant.

02

点植物卡，再点空草格。Tap a plant card, then an empty tile.

先点太阳芽卡片，再点教学提示亮起的草格。太阳芽会不断产出新的阳光。卡片上的数字就是种下它需要的阳光。Choose the Sunbud card, then tap the tile highlighted by the tutorial. Sunbuds keep producing more sun. The number on each card is its planting cost.

卡片显示倒计时？等它准备好再种。阳光不够？继续收集，界面会告诉你还差多少。A countdown on the card means it is recharging. Need more sun? Keep collecting; the game shows how much you are short.

03

把射手种在僵尸来的那一行。Put shooters in the lane under attack.

选择种子投手，再点与僵尸同一行的空草格。射手会自动向右攻击，你不需要一直点它。第一关按提示在中间一行种两株投手。Choose a Seed Slinger, then an empty tile in the same horizontal lane as a zombie. Shooters attack to the right automatically. In the first garden, follow the prompts to plant two in the middle lane.

示意一行的位置关系。新的一行开放后，也要为它安排防守。A simple lane layout. When another lane opens, give it defenders too.

04

边收阳光，边补上防守。Keep collecting. Keep defending.

先让后方的太阳芽持续产阳光，再给有僵尸的一行补射手。解锁树皮盾后，把它种在射手右边，让它先替射手挡住僵尸。植物会自动攻击或工作，你负责选择位置和时机。Keep your Sunbuds producing, then add shooters where zombies appear. Once you unlock a shield plant, place it to the right of your shooters to take the bites first. Plants work automatically; you choose where and when to plant.

玩熟以后，再学这三件事。Three things to try next.

05

等植物长大，再选择开花。Let plants mature, then choose a bloom.

刚种下的是幼芽，比较脆弱，需要保护。通常存活 40 秒会自动长大。若铲子已选中，先再点一下铲子，或选回任意植物卡，再点已经长大的植物，查看两种开花选择和各自费用；选中一种并支付阳光，它会变得更强。New plants start as fragile sprouts. Most mature automatically after surviving 40 seconds. If the shovel is selected, tap it again or choose a plant card first. Then tap a mature plant to see its two bloom choices and their sun cost. Choose one and spend the sun to make it stronger.

查看植物详情时，战斗会暂停。可以慢慢读，关闭后再继续。一次性爆炸植物不需要等它长大。The battle pauses while you read plant details. Take your time, then close to continue. Instant explosive plants do not need to mature.

06

想换打法，先调整植物卡。Change your cards to try a new plan.

在开局或菜单中点“选择植物”。先点上方要替换的编号卡槽，再点下方一株已解锁植物，新卡会直接放进这个位置。拿不准时，可以用推荐按钮一键配好，再确认出战。Tap “Choose plants” before starting or from the menu. Tap a numbered slot at the top, then an unlocked plant below to replace that card. Unsure what to bring? Use the recommendation button, then confirm your squad.

调整阵容后进入花园，会从本关开头重新开始，不会把新植物直接换进正在进行的战斗。每日挑战使用固定阵容。Entering the garden with a changed squad starts this level again from the beginning. It does not replace plants in the current battle. Daily challenges use a fixed squad.

07

用铲子腾出位置，夜里记得带灯。Make space with the shovel. Bring light at night.

铲子解锁后，先点铲子，再点要移除的植物；移除不会返还阳光。夜战没有天降阳光，要带产阳光植物和灯笼花，让敌人进入光照范围，射手才能准确攻击。Once the shovel unlocks, choose it and tap a plant to remove it. Removed plants do not refund sun. At night no sun falls from the sky: bring sun producers and a lantern to reveal enemies so your shooters can aim.

卡住了？先看这里。Stuck? Start here.

点了草格，为什么没种下？Why did nothing plant when I tapped?

先确认选中了植物卡。再检查阳光是否足够、卡片倒计时是否结束、草格是否空着。有石头、尚未开放或暂时被水淹的格子不能直接种植。第一关还需要按照亮起的提示操作。Make sure a plant card is selected, you have enough sun, its recharge is finished, and the tile is empty. Stones, unopened lanes and temporarily flooded tiles prevent planting. In the first garden, follow the highlighted tutorial prompts.

僵尸总是从某一行冲过去，怎么办？Zombies keep getting through one lane. What should I try?

先给那一行补射手，再把挡路植物放到射手右边。不要把全部阳光都花在同一行。教学关没有救场割草机；之后的普通关卡每行有一次救场机会，用完就没有了。Add shooters to that lane and put a shield to their right. Spread your sun across every open lane. The tutorial has no emergency mower; later normal levels give each lane one rescue, which can only be used once.

什么是“开花方向”？What is a bloom choice?

就是同一株植物的两种升级方式。例如种子投手可以选更强的打击，也可以选能穿透多个敌人的攻击。先取消铲子或选回植物卡，再点植物打开详情，阅读两张卡片，选适合当前情况的一种；一株植物只能开花一次。It is one of two upgrades for the same plant. A Seed Slinger can gain stronger hits or attacks that pass through more enemies. Deselect the shovel or choose a plant card, then tap the plant to read its two upgrade cards and choose what helps this garden. Each plant can bloom only once.

怎么暂停，或者让游戏轻松一点？How do I pause or make the game easier?

点暂停按钮可暂停或继续战斗。想调整难度、速度和声音，点“菜单”按钮，或按 Esc 打开菜单；选择休闲难度后，可以开启自动收集阳光。如果植物详情或对话框已打开，Esc 会先关闭它；在全屏中会先退出全屏。离开页面前，战斗也会暂停并保存在当前设备。Use the pause button to pause or resume the battle. To adjust difficulty, speed and sound, tap Menu or press Esc to open it. Casual difficulty also supports automatic sun collection. Esc first closes any open plant details or dialog, or exits full screen. Leaving the page pauses and saves this device’s battle.

用键盘也很顺手。Keyboard, if you prefer.
