收阳光。种植物。
守住左边的家。Collect. Plant.
Protect your home.

僵尸从右边走来，植物会自动攻击。你只需要决定：种什么，种在哪，什么时候补上防守。Zombies approach from the right. Your plants attack automatically. You decide what to plant, where to put it, and when to add more defence.

过关条件，先看这一句。The goal of each garden.

击败全部波次，别让僵尸闯进左边的家。有些关卡还有额外目标，开战前先看本关说明。星级是额外评价，不必拿满三星才能继续。Stop every wave before zombies reach the house on the left. Some gardens have an extra objective; read it before starting. Stars are an extra rating: you do not need all three to continue.

第一关，跟着亮起的提示做。Your first garden: follow the highlights.

01

点发光的阳光。Tap a glowing sun.

金色阳光就是种植费用。点草坪上的阳光收集；之后也可以点“收集阳光”一次收走全部。Golden sun pays for plants. Tap a sun on the lawn to collect it. You can also use “Collect sun” to gather every sun at once.

02

点“太阳芽”，再点亮起的草格。Choose Sunbud, then the highlighted tile.

先选卡片，再选位置，才会种下植物。太阳芽会持续产阳光，让你买得起更多植物。卡片上的数字是价格。Planting takes two taps: choose a card, then a tile. Sunbuds keep producing sun so you can afford more plants. The number on a card is its price.

03

在中间一行，种两株“种子投手”。Plant two Seed Slingers in the middle lane.

继续收阳光，点“种子投手”，再点中间一行亮起的空格。种完一株，等卡片冷却结束，再种第二株。它们会自动向右射击。Keep collecting sun. Choose Seed Slinger and tap a highlighted empty tile in the middle lane. Wait for the card to recharge, then plant the second one. They fire to the right automatically.

卡片倒计时还没结束，或阳光不够，就暂时种不了。看提示，等准备好再点。You cannot plant while the card is recharging or you are short of sun. Check the message and try when it is ready.

04

守住第一波，继续收阳光。Defend the first wave. Keep collecting.

射手只负责自己的横向路线。看到哪一行来了僵尸，就为那一行补防守。第一关没有救场割草机，要护住两株投手。Shooters cover their own horizontal lane. Add defenders to the lane where zombies appear. The tutorial has no emergency mower, so protect both shooters.

05

第一波后，铲掉亮起的练习芽。After the first wave, remove the practice bud.

游戏会提示你点“铲子”，再点亮起的练习芽。只铲提示的那一株，保留你种下的防线。完成后继续守住剩余波次，第一关就学会了。When prompted, choose “Shovel”, then tap the highlighted practice bud. Remove that one and keep your defenders. Finish the remaining waves to complete your first garden.

下一关，再多会一点。A few tools for the next garden.

06

开战前，先看关卡，再换阵容。Read the garden, then choose your squad.

在“花园旅程”逐关解锁下一关，也可以重玩已完成的关卡。开战前先读关卡简报，看看本关目标、预计波次和阵容。换卡时，点上方要换的编号卡槽，再点下方已解锁植物；也可用“推荐阵容”先配好。Unlock missions in order in Garden journey, or replay a completed garden. Before starting, read the mission briefing for your goal, waves and squad. To change plants, tap a numbered squad slot, then an unlocked plant below. “Recommended squad” can fill a squad for you.

找不到植物时，可搜索名字或能力，如“减速”，也可按职能筛选。更换阵容后进入花园，会从本关开头重新开始。每日挑战使用固定阵容。Search by name or ability, such as “slow”, or filter by role to find a plant. Entering with a changed squad starts the garden again from the beginning. Daily challenges use a fixed squad.

07

植物长大后，点它选择开花。Tap a mature plant to choose a bloom.

新植物是比较脆弱的幼芽，通常存活40秒会自动长大。点成株，读两种开花能力与费用，再选一种；每株只能开花一次。查看详情时战斗会暂停，可以慢慢读。New plants start as fragile sprouts. Most mature after surviving 40 seconds. Tap a mature plant, compare its two bloom abilities and costs, then choose one. Each plant can bloom once. The battle pauses while you read its details.

如果铲子已选中，先选回任意植物卡，再点你想开花的植物。一次性爆炸植物不需要等它长大。If the shovel is selected, choose any plant card first, then tap the plant you want to bloom. Instant explosives do not need to mature.

08

某一路撑不住，用雨露争取时间。Use Dewfall to give a lane breathing room.

先看“战场一览”：每一路显示可见敌人的数量，接近园门时会提醒。教学完成后，点选需要帮助的一路，再点“施放雨露”。它不花阳光：为本路受伤植物恢复30%最大生命，并让看得见的地面敌人减速5秒。接着补上射手或盾，才能稳住防线。Garden at a glance shows the number of visible enemies in each lane and warns when they approach the gate. After the tutorial, choose a lane there, then tap “Cast Dewfall”. It costs no sun: injured plants in that lane recover 30% of their maximum health, and visible ground enemies slow for 5 seconds. Follow up with shooters or shields to hold the lane.

开局充能15秒，使用后冷却45秒。不能复活植物，也不影响飞行、地下或夜雾中未被照亮的敌人。没有受伤植物和可减速敌人的路线不会消耗充能。压力提示只统计可见敌人，夜雾中仍要留意未照亮的地方。It charges for 15 seconds at the start and recharges for 45 seconds after use. It cannot revive plants or slow flying, underground or unlit enemies in night fog. A lane with no injured plants or eligible enemies does not consume the charge. The overview counts visible enemies only; stay alert to unlit areas in night fog.

09

结束一局，先看哪里需要改。After a battle, choose one thing to improve.

看“这一场，发生了什么”：守护时间、击退数量、开花次数、阳光收获和完成波次。失败时再读改进建议。若某一路太薄，下一局就早点补射手；若一直没阳光，就早点种太阳芽。不必一次改完所有阵容，先解决最明显的缺口。Read “Your garden, reviewed” for time defended, visitors defeated, blooms, sun harvested and waves completed. After a defeat, read the advice too. If one lane was weak, add shooters there earlier. If you kept running out of sun, plant Sunbuds earlier. Start by fixing the clearest gap.

点了没反应？在这里查。Something not working? Check here.

点草格，为什么没种下？Why will a tile not accept my plant?

先选一张植物卡。再检查阳光是否够、卡片是否冷却完成、格子是否空着。石块、尚未开放或暂时被水淹的格子不能直接种。第一关要按亮起的提示操作；有植物上限或阳光预算的关卡，也要遵守本关目标。Choose a plant card first. Check that you have enough sun, the card has recharged and the tile is empty. Stones, closed lanes and temporary flooding block planting. Follow the highlighted steps in the tutorial. Gardens with a plant limit or sun budget also restrict planting.

夜里为什么射手不攻击？Why are my shooters not firing at night?

夜雾中的敌人需要先被灯笼花照亮，射手才能瞄准。夜战没有天降阳光，要带产阳光植物和灯笼花。遇到飞行、地下等特殊敌人，去图鉴查看对应的应对办法。Lanterns must reveal enemies in night fog before shooters can aim at them. No sun falls from the sky at night: bring sun producers and a lantern. For flying, underground or other special enemies, check the field guide for counters.

铲子会退阳光吗？石头怎么清？Does the shovel refund sun? How do I clear a stone?

铲子解锁后，点铲子，再点要移除的植物，不会退还阳光。清理石块需要50阳光。有些目标植物不能移除。想重新种植，记得先选回植物卡。Once the shovel unlocks, choose it and tap a plant to remove it. Plants do not refund sun. Clearing a stone costs 50 sun. Protected objective plants cannot be removed. Choose a plant card again when you want to plant.

想暂停，或让游戏轻松一点？How do I pause or make the game easier?

点暂停按钮可暂停或继续。打开“暂停菜单”可调难度、速度和声音；休闲难度可以开启自动收集阳光。切换难度会重试本关。进度保存在当前设备；换设备前可在菜单中导出存档码。Use the pause button to pause or resume. The pause menu has difficulty, speed and sound settings. Casual difficulty supports automatic sun collection. Changing difficulty restarts the garden. Progress is saved on this device; export a save code from the menu before moving devices.
