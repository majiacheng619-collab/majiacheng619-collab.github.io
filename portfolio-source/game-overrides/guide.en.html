THE STUBBORN GARDEN

这座倔强院子的指南

第一关会牵着你走完这些步骤。之后，院子一座一座开放。The first garden walks you through these steps. Every garden after it opens in order.

每一株，都值得好好长大。Every sprout deserves to grow.

从收集第一束阳光，到点亮最后一片夜雾。先学会保护，再找到你的布阵节奏。From your first sun to the last patch of night fog. Learn to protect, then find your own rhythm.

01

先收一束阳光Collect the first sun

点击亮着的阳光，把它收进左上角的计数器。Tap the glowing sun to move it into the counter.

02

选择太阳芽Choose a Sunbud

点太阳芽种子袋，再点中间亮着的草格。Tap the Sunbud packet, then the glowing middle tile.

03

种两株射手Plant two shooters

在中路种下两株种子投手，单株守不住第一波。Plant two Seed Slingers in the middle lane; one cannot hold the first wave.

04

护芽四十秒Protect forty seconds

萌芽便宜、半输出、被啃即死；除即爆的爆爆菇外，存活四十秒长成成株。Cheap sprouts have half output and die from one bite; survive forty seconds to mature, except the instant Pop Cap.

05

让它开花Let it bloom

点成株查看，再按 U 或点开花按钮，在两个开花方向中选择，取舍伤害、生命、光照和控制。Inspect a mature plant, then use U or Bloom to choose one of two blooms, trading damage, HP, light and control.

06

守住屋前Hold the porch

教学关没有救场割草机，僵尸到屋前就失败；其他关每路仅救场一次。The tutorial has no mower: reaching the house loses; later each lane has one rescue.

07

铲掉练习芽Practice the shovel

第一波后点铲子，再铲掉右边亮着的练习芽；不会返还阳光。After wave one, tap the shovel and remove the glowing practice bud; sun is not refunded.

08

给夜雾一盏灯Light the night fog

夜战没有天降阳光，带产出和光源，灯照到的敌人才可被准确攻击。Night has no sky sun; bring income and light so attackers can target lit enemies.

下一座院子会问什么？What will the next garden ask?

清晨阳光多，正午来客快，黄昏天降阳光减半，夜战没有天降阳光。新敌人会提前一波用剪影预警，大波提前三秒举旗；首次真正遇见会有三秒提示。Morning brings extra sun; noon brings faster visitors; dusk halves sky sun; night brings none. New visitors get a silhouette one wave early, flags appear three seconds early, and first encounters show a short tip.

点种子袋再点草格；点僵尸标记集火。键位可在暂停菜单改，Esc 打开菜单，触屏可直接点开花按钮。Tap a seed then a tile; tap a zombie to focus fire. Rebind keys in the pause menu, use Esc for the menu, or tap Bloom on touchscreens.
