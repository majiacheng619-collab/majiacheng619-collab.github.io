当前版本 7.0.0。An overview of the mechanics and tools you can use in the game today.

7.0.0 · 2026.10.19

从准备到复盘，守护更有方向。A clearer plan, from briefing to review.

“花园旅程”展示四个世界的进度：完成前一关后解锁下一关，已完成关卡可以重玩。开战前的关卡简报集中呈现本关目标、进攻波次、来客与出战阵容；尚未遇见的敌人保留为未知来客。Garden journey shows progress across four worlds. Complete a mission to unlock the next, or replay a completed garden. The briefing brings together the goal, waves, expected visitors and equipped squad. Enemies you have not met remain unknown visitors.

阵容可以按名字或能力搜索，并按植物职能筛选。先点上方卡槽，再点下方植物即可替换；应用新阵容会重新开始当前关卡。Search squad plants by name or ability and filter by role. Choose a slot, then a plant to replace it. Applying a new squad restarts the current garden.

“战场一览”按路线显示可见来客数量与接近园门的提醒，也可直接收集阳光、查看可开花植物。它不会显示夜雾中尚未照亮的敌人。Garden at a glance shows visible visitor counts by lane and warns when they approach the gate. You can also collect sun or review bloom-ready plants there. It does not reveal enemies hidden in night fog.

新增“战术雨露”：选择一路后免费施放，为本路存活的受伤植物恢复30%最大生命，最多恢复至满血，并让可见地面敌人减速5秒。开局充能15秒，施放后冷却45秒。教学期间不可用，不会复活植物，也不影响飞行、地下或未被照亮的敌人；没有可治疗或减速目标时不消耗充能。Tactical Dewfall is a free lane rescue: heal living, injured plants by 30% of maximum health, up to full health, and slow visible ground enemies for 5 seconds. It charges for 15 seconds at the start and recharges for 45 seconds after use. It is unavailable during the tutorial, cannot revive plants and does not affect flying, underground or unlit enemies. A lane with no eligible targets does not consume the charge.

战后复盘呈现守护时间、击退来客、植物开花、阳光收获、守门员出动和完成波次，并在失败后给出建议。“怎么玩”按第一关的实际顺序重写，包括第一波后的铲子练习：只移除亮起的练习芽，保留自己种下的防线。The battle review shows time defended, visitors defeated, blooms, sun harvested, guards used and waves completed, with advice after a defeat. The guide follows the actual first-garden sequence, including the shovel lesson after wave one: remove only the highlighted practice bud and keep your defenders.

先保护，再开花Protect, then bloom

萌芽便宜，但输出较低、也更脆弱。守护它长大后，选择两种开花方向之一。Sprouts cost less but have reduced output and are fragile. Protect them until maturity, then choose one of two blooms.

四个世界，逐关推进Four worlds, in sequence

清晨、正午、黄昏与夜战改变阳光、敌人节奏和可见范围。植物与来客随着冒险逐步出现。Morning, noon, dusk and night change sun, enemy pace and visibility. Meet new plants and visitors as the adventure unfolds.

按自己的方式操作Play your way

支持触屏点选、键位设置、音量调整与后台暂停。需要休息时，打开菜单即可暂停。Tap controls, key bindings, volume settings and background pausing let you play at your own pace. Open the menu to pause.

带走你的院子Take your garden with you

在设置中导出、导入存档码，并保留最近的自动备份。当前进度以本机存档为准。Export and import save codes in Settings, with recent automatic backups. Your local save holds your current progress.
