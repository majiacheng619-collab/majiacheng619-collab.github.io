这里记录当前可体验的玩法与使用方式。每一项说明，都对应你在游戏中能找到的实际功能。An overview of the mechanics and tools you can use in the game today.

6.3.0 · 2026.10.17

每一次触碰，都有回应。Every touch has a response.

我重新整理了战斗与触屏操作：僵尸接触植物后，先准备再啃咬；升级面板独立滚动，查看分支时暂停战斗；取消或滑动手势不会误种植物。图鉴、指南、设置和结算沿用同一套界面与萌芽标识。I rebuilt bite timing and touch controls. Visitors wind up before a bite; a scrollable bloom sheet freezes battle while you read. Cancelled or scrolling gestures do not plant. The field guide, settings and results share one visual system.

旧进度和战局继续保留。由于战斗规则变化，迁移中的历史战局不生成回放；新开战局使用 6.3.0 规则。I preserve existing progress and battles. Migrated battles cannot produce a reproducible replay after this rule change; new battles use 6.3.0.

先保护，再开花Protect, then bloom

萌芽便宜，但输出较低、也更脆弱。守护它长大后，选择两种开花方向之一。Sprouts cost less but have reduced output and are fragile. Protect them until maturity, then choose one of two blooms.

四个世界，逐关推进Four worlds, in sequence

清晨、正午、黄昏与夜战改变阳光、敌人节奏和可见范围。植物与来客随着冒险逐步出现。Morning, noon, dusk and night change sun, enemy pace and visibility. Meet new plants and visitors as the adventure unfolds.

按自己的方式操作Play your way

支持触屏点选、键位设置、音量调整与后台暂停。需要休息时，打开菜单即可暂停。Tap controls, key bindings, volume settings and background pausing let you play at your own pace. Open the menu to pause.

带走你的院子Take your garden with you

在设置中导出、导入存档码，并保留最近的自动备份。当前进度以本机存档为准。Export and import save codes in Settings, with recent automatic backups. Your local save holds your current progress.
